To My Favourite Person, ❤️

Aaj humara one month complete ho gaya... aur sach bolu toh pata hi nahi chala ki ye ek mahina itni jaldi kaise nikal gaya.

Jab maine socha bhi nahi tha ki mujhe girlfriend chahiye, us waqt Bhagwan ji ne hum dono ko unexpectedly mila diya. Jo bhi humne saath mein experience kiya, woh bahut achha raha. Meri life mein aapke jaisa importance kisi ne nahi diya. Meri life mein bahut kuch ho chuka tha, par aapke aane ke baad har cheez achhi hone lagi. ❤️

I never knew that someone could become this important to me in such a short time. But today, when I think about you, I realize that this one month has given me something I had almost stopped believing in again—something genuine, peaceful, and special. ❤️

I have learned so many things from my past. I learned that words are easy, but actions matter. I learned that trust takes time, that communication matters, and that you should never take someone’s presence for granted. Most importantly, I learned what it feels like to lose something you truly valued, and because of that, I understand even more how precious it is to have someone who genuinely cares.

And then you came into my life. 🥺❤️

You didn’t suddenly erase my past, but you made me look at love differently. You made me believe that maybe something beautiful can still be built when two people are honest, patient, caring, and willing to understand each other.

You are very special to me. Sometimes I call you my “last hope,” not because I want to put any pressure on you, but because after everything I’ve learned, I want to give this relationship my most honest heart. I don’t want to repeat old mistakes. I want to learn, grow, communicate, and make the little moments with you matter. 🫂❤️

This is just our first month... ek chhoti si beginning of something that has already become very precious to me.

Thank you for choosing me, thank you for staying, and thank you for making this one month so beautiful.

Happy One Month, My Love. ❤️

I hope this is just the beginning of many more beautiful memories together.

One month down... hopefully many more to go. 🫶🏻❤️

— Yours, always ❤️
